About

In security operations, the job is to turn a noisy alert into a clear answer: what happened, how far it spread, and what to do about it. I triage SIEM and EDR alerts, analyze malware and network traffic, investigate phishing and BEC, and map attacker behavior to MITRE ATT&CK.

I came up through IT support, systems administration, and compliance, so I understand the environments I defend and the documentation that audits and leadership need. I’ve worked across government, legal, mortgage, retail, managed-service, and aviation organizations.

What I do

Incident response and alert triage
Severity assessment, containment, eradication, and recovery, with timelines, indicators of compromise, and lessons learned documented for every incident.
Phishing and BEC investigation
Email headers, sender domains, URLs, attachments, and account activity, followed by message containment and account-protection recommendations.
Forensics and threat mapping
Malware, network traffic, and endpoint analysis, with observed attacker behavior mapped to MITRE ATT&CK for classification and reporting.
Vulnerability management and compliance
Nessus and Vicarius vRx scanning with CVSS prioritization. NIST, ISO 27001, CMMC, and SPRS documentation, including System Security Plans.
